Coaching

Depth. Threshold. Return.

Depth guidance for the well and reaching — for the soul approaching a threshold, crossing it, or finding the ground on the far side.

Who it's for

For the well, and reaching.

This is for people who are well, not unwell — and reaching for something conventional therapy isn't built to reach.

You've done the therapy. You're not in crisis. You're standing at an edge — a calling half-heard, a life that no longer fits, a threshold you can feel but can't yet name — and you want someone who won't pathologize the reaching, who can meet you in the largeness of the question instead of shrinking it back to a symptom.

How it works

The work that begins where therapy ends.

Therapy tends the wound and brings the exile home. This work lifts the whole self toward what it's becoming.

The two are different in kind, not degree — one restores, the other reaches. And this work is held, throughout, by a clinical eye: if what you're facing is better served by treatment, you'll be told plainly and pointed well. Depth and safety, which few offer together.

The method itself stays quiet. There are no stages to climb, no ladder, nothing to master or perform. Only your own life, met closely, and carried as far as you're ready to go.

The shape of it

As long as the work asks for.

It takes the shape the moment requires — a single conversation, or a long walk through a season of change. You choose the depth; the door stays open at every one of them.

The Session
A single conversation, for when one thing needs to be seen clearly.
The Short Arc
Three sessions, to move through a specific threshold from near side to far.
The Season
Twelve weekly sessions — a true season, one turn of the year, for a passage that needs sustained tending.
The Long Walk
Twenty-six sessions across a full year, met every other week — the deep remaking, walked at a stride you can keep.
